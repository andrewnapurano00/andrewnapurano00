## About me

My work brings together **data engineering, financial analysis, applied machine learning, and agentic AI**. I focus on the full path from source data to research output: API ingestion, governed lakehouse datasets, exploratory analysis, time-aware model evaluation, and interactive financial research tools.

I'm interested in making financial research more reproducible and understandable. That means tracking where data comes from, respecting when information became available, testing assumptions, and communicating uncertainty alongside results.

## Featured work

### 📈 [S&P 500 ML](https://github.com/andrewnapurano00/SP500-ML) · Work in progress

A research project exploring whether technical, sector, and financial-statement features improve next-month S&P 500 stock ranking. Includes executive EDA, 12 matched model variants, purged walk-forward validation, calibration diagnostics, and portfolio comparisons against VOO. Published results include uncertainty and failed controls, with a private batch-scoring candidate and a roadmap toward deployment.

**Skills demonstrated:** hypothesis design, feature engineering, temporal validation, statistical inference, model evaluation, and reproducible experimentation.

## How I approach data work

- **Start with the question:** define the financial decision, target, and evaluation before choosing a model.
- **Build a dependable foundation:** preserve lineage, validate joins, and make data timing and applicability explicit.
- **Evaluate honestly:** use time-aware comparisons, report uncertainty, and retain evidence that challenges a hypothesis.
- **Keep AI grounded:** connect analysis to available sources, distinguish facts from assumptions, and expose evidence gaps.
- **Improve deliberately:** treat testing, documentation, monitoring, and deployment readiness as continuing work.
